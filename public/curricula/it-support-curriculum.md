# Learn IT Support

> From ticket triage to trusted end-user problem solving

A three-level curriculum for IT support engineers — diagnosing desktop and identity issues, delivering calm customer service, and escalating with enough detail that engineering and ops can finish the job.

## Prerequisites

- Comfort helping people with computers and common apps
- Basic understanding of accounts, passwords, and Wi‑Fi
- Patience and clear written communication

## Tools & Technologies

- ServiceNow / Jira Service Management / Zendesk
- Active Directory / Entra ID (Azure AD)
- Google Workspace / Microsoft 365
- Jamf / Intune / MDM
- Remote desktop / Screen share
- Password managers / SSO (Okta)
- Slack / Teams
- Knowledge base (Confluence / Notion)
- Antivirus / EDR consoles

## Support Foundations — Beginner

**Goal:** Triage tickets, resolve common access and device issues, and document clearly
**Duration:** 4–6 wks

### Service Desk Basics

#### Ticket hygiene

- Priority, category, and SLAs
- Gathering repro steps and environment details
- Closing notes users can understand

#### Customer communication

- Empathy without overpromising
- Setting expectations on time-to-fix
- Escalation language that stays professional

### Identity & Access

#### Accounts & SSO

- Password resets and MFA resets safely
- SSO vs. local app accounts
- Least privilege: when not to grant access

#### Email & collaboration

- Mailbox access, groups, and shared drives
- Calendar and meeting room basics
- Spam/phish reporting workflows

### Device Troubleshooting

#### Desktop & laptop

- Boot, disk, and update issues
- Printer and peripheral basics
- VPN and Wi‑Fi connectivity checks

### Project Work

- Resolve a mock ticket queue (10 tickets) with proper notes
- Write KB articles for password reset and VPN connect
- Identity checklist: joiners, movers, leavers for one role
- Phishing report drill with escalation to security

## Reliable Operations Support — Intermediate

**Goal:** Own MDM, endpoint health, and escalations with strong diagnostic depth
**Duration:** 6–8 wks

### Endpoint Management

#### MDM & compliance

- Enrolling and wiping managed devices
- Compliance policies and encryption
- Software distribution and patch windows

#### Security hygiene

- EDR alerts that support should act on
- Lost/stolen device playbooks
- USB and data-handling policies

### Deeper Diagnostics

#### Networking for support

- DNS, DHCP, and proxy symptoms
- Traceroute and ping without overclaiming
- When to hand off to network engineering

#### App & SSO failures

- SAML/OIDC error reading at a support level
- Browser profiles, cache, and extensions
- Reproducing issues remotely

### Process Quality

#### Knowledge & trends

- Turning repeat tickets into KB and automation
- Weekly incident themes for IT leadership
- Major incident comms for end users

### Project Work

- MDM enrollment + compliance guide for macOS or Windows
- Root-cause write-up for a recurring VPN/SSO outage
- Major-incident user comms pack (start, update, resolve)
- Ticket trend report with 3 proposed permanent fixes

## Support Leadership & Systems — Expert

**Goal:** Design support operations, improve SLAs, and partner on identity and endpoint strategy
**Duration:** 6–8 wks

### Support Operations

#### Queue & SLA design

- Tiering L1/L2/L3 and routing rules
- Measuring CSAT, FCR, and reopen rates
- On-call rotations for IT support

#### Automation

- Self-service password and access portals
- Chatbots and forms that collect the right data
- Scripted remediations that stay safe

### Enterprise Partnerships

#### Identity & lifecycle

- Joiner-mover-leaver automation with HR
- Group strategy and access reviews
- Vendor and contractor access patterns

#### Workplace technology

- Hardware standards and refresh planning
- Office AV and hybrid meeting reliability
- Budget awareness for licenses and devices

### Leadership

#### Coaching & influence

- Training L1 analysts and shadowing quality
- Partnering with security without blocking users
- Executive updates during company-wide outages

### Project Work

- L1/L2/L3 playbook with routing and escalation SLAs
- Joiner-mover-leaver design with HR and identity owners
- Self-service catalog proposal that cuts top 5 ticket types
- Quarterly IT support OKRs with baseline metrics
