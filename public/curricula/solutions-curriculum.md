# Learn Solutions Engineering

> From discovery demos to architectures customers will buy and run

A three-level curriculum for solutions engineers — translating customer problems into credible demos, technical designs, and mutual success plans that win deals and survive implementation.

## Prerequisites

- Solid technical foundation in at least one domain (APIs, cloud, data, or security)
- Comfort presenting to mixed technical and business audiences
- Willingness to learn products deeply and admit what you do not know

## Tools & Technologies

- CRM (Salesforce / HubSpot)
- Demo environments / sandboxes
- Diagramming (draw.io / Lucidchart / Excalidraw)
- Postman / sample APIs
- Slide decks & live demos
- Notion / Confluence
- Zoom / Meet
- Proposal & RFP tools
- Observability dashboards (for POV metrics)
- Git (for demo repos)

## SE Foundations — Beginner

**Goal:** Run discovery, deliver a crisp product demo, and capture requirements accurately
**Duration:** 4–6 wks

### Customer Discovery

#### Asking the right questions

- Business outcomes vs. feature shopping
- Stakeholders, buying process, and timeline
- Current state, constraints, and success metrics

#### Qualification partnership

- Working with AEs without becoming a salesperson-only
- Technical go/no-go signals
- Documenting assumptions and open questions

### Demo Craft

#### Story-driven demos

- Persona-based scripts, not feature tours
- Environment prep and failure recovery
- Timeboxing and leaving room for questions

#### Technical credibility

- Explaining APIs, auth, and data flow simply
- Handling “how do you integrate with X?”
- Parking lot for deep-dives

### Artifacts

#### Write-ups that stick

- Discovery notes and mutual action plans
- Simple architecture sketches
- Follow-up emails that advance the deal

### Project Work

- Discovery call role-play with notes and success criteria
- 15-minute persona demo with backup plan for a failure
- One-page solution sketch for a sample customer problem
- Mutual action plan template filled for a mock opportunity

## Solution Design & POVs — Intermediate

**Goal:** Own technical evaluations, POCs/POVs, and integration designs that de-risk purchase
**Duration:** 6–8 wks

### Solution Architecture

#### Designing fit

- Reference architectures tailored to the customer
- Security, privacy, and compliance talking points
- Sizing, performance, and cost ranges

#### Integrations

- Auth patterns (SSO, SCIM, API keys)
- Webhooks, ETL, and event-driven options
- Data mapping workshops

### Proofs & Evaluations

#### POV / POC management

- Scoped success criteria before you start
- Environment access, sample data, and timeline
- Scoring results and recommending next steps

#### RFPs & security reviews

- Answering technical questionnaires accurately
- Partnering with security and product for edge cases
- Competitive differentiation without trash talk

### Multi-Threading

#### Working the account

- Champion vs. economic buyer vs. blocker
- Workshops for architects and operators
- Internal handoff to onboarding/CS

### Project Work

- Customer-specific architecture + integration sequence diagram
- POV plan with success metrics, timeline, and owners
- Completed sample security/RFP questionnaire pack
- Workshop deck for a technical evaluation committee

## Strategic Solutions Leadership — Expert

**Goal:** Shape complex deals, product feedback loops, and reusable SE playbooks at scale
**Duration:** 6–8 wks

### Complex Deals

#### Enterprise pursuits

- Multi-product and partner solutions
- Phased rollout and value realization plans
- Executive briefings that stay technical enough

#### Risk & negotiation support

- Contractual SLAs and technical feasibility
- Custom work vs. roadmap influence
- Saying no while preserving the relationship

### Scale the Practice

#### SE systems

- Demo environments and reusable assets
- Win/loss technical retrospectives
- Onboarding new SEs and certification paths

#### Product partnership

- Structured product feedback from the field
- Beta and design-partner programs
- Competitive intelligence that product can use

### Leadership

#### Influence

- Coaching AEs on technical discovery
- Prioritizing SE time across a pipeline
- Building trust as the technical voice of the customer

### Project Work

- Enterprise pursuit pack: architecture, POV, exec brief, MAP
- SE playbook (discovery, demo, POV, handoff) for your product
- Win/loss technical retro with product recommendations
- Reusable demo environment + asset library design
